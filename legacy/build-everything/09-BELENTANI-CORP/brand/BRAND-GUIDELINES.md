# Belentani Corp — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

**Company:** Belentani Corp  
**Legal Name:** Belentani · estudi digital  
**Tagline:** "Building systems that protect people and create experiences."  
**Founded:** 2021  
**Location:** L'Hospitalet de Llobregat, Barcelona, Spain  

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
- Text-based: "BELENTANI" in Space Grotesk 700
- Monogram: "BC" in a rounded square
- Icon: Abstract geometric mark combining code brackets < > with a shield

### Color Variations
- Full color (dark background)
- Reversed (light background)
- Monochrome (single color)
- Favicon (16x16, 32x32)

### Clear Space
- Minimum clear space = height of the "B" in the logo on all sides

## Voice & Tone

### Professional
- Direct, no filler
- Technical precision
- Evidence-based claims

### Confident
- "I build systems that protect people"
- "6.7x faster. 8.4x more efficient."
- Numbers over adjectives

### Human
- First person
- Conversational but not casual
- Honest about limitations
